## But... why?

It's cool, and it's really handy for keeping track of big projects in your survival worlds, or on servers with friends.

---

## How do I use it?

Noteable needs [OneConfig][oneconfig]. Open the OneConfig menu and pick Notes in the sidebar, or run `/noteable` (or `/notes`). From there you can write, edit, pin, copy and delete notes.

The editor's toolbar can drop your coordinates, dimension, biome, facing, world or server, player name or the date in at the cursor. It can also add checklist items, bullets and dividers.

Pin a note and it gets its own HUD. Move, resize and style it in the OneConfig HUD editor like any other HUD. Checklists show up as real checkboxes, and you can tick them right on the HUD while chat is open.

---

## Do my noteses save?

Yep. Each note is its own file in `config/Noteable`, and everything is still there when you restart your game.

---

## What versions does it support?

Minecraft 1.21.1 to 26.3 on Fabric, and 1.8.9 on Ornithe.
