- Noteable now runs on OneConfig and needs it installed. All of its settings are in the OneConfig menu.
- Added a Notes page to the OneConfig sidebar, where you write, edit, pin, copy and delete notes.
- Each pinned note is its own HUD. Move, resize and style it in the OneConfig HUD editor like any other HUD. You can duplicate a HUD to show a note twice, or point it at a different note in its settings.
- Notes can have an icon, which their HUD shows beside the title.
- The editor's toolbar inserts your coordinates, dimension, biome, facing, world or server, player name or the current date at the cursor.
- The toolbar also adds checklist items, bullets and dividers. HUDs draw them as real checkboxes, bullets and lines, and you can tick a checklist item on the HUD while chat is open.
- Notes remember when they were created and last edited.
- Added `/noteable new`, `list`, `open`, `pin`, `copy` and `folder`, with `/notes` as a shorter alias.
- Each note is now saved as its own file in `config/Noteable`. Notes from older versions move over on first launch, and the ones you had pinned get a HUD.
- Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 to 26.3 on Fabric and 1.8.9 on Ornithe. Forge, NeoForge and Minecraft 1.12.2 to 1.20.6 are no longer supported.
